# On-call severity policy

Severity is assigned by deterministic code from observed user impact, signal magnitude and evidence quality. It is not inferred from an alert name or an agent's confidence. Record the Prometheus query/time range or trace IDs supporting each input. A missing/stale signal is unknown, not zero.

| Level | Trigger (all stated conditions unless “or”) | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **SEV1** | Broad critical-path error rate ≥5%, or critical consumer lag ≥1,000, when impact is sustained in fresh telemetry or its confirmed fault flag is active. Any qualifying broad critical-path error rate requires at least 20 requests in the rolling 10-minute sample. At least two independent evidence items are required. | Page primary and incident commander immediately; investigate concurrently; propose one scoped, allow-listed fix at a time. |
| **SEV2** | Broad or partial user impact with error rate ≥1%, or critical-path p95 latency ≥500 ms, when impact is sustained in fresh telemetry or its confirmed fault flag is active. At least two independent evidence items are required. | Page the responsible service owner; maintain incident updates; prioritize mitigation and verify recovery. |
| **SEV3** | Localized/low-impact issue, an impact below SEV2 thresholds, or fewer than two independent evidence items. | Service owner investigates during normal response; continue read-only evidence gathering where causation is uncertain. |
| **NONE** | No fault is confirmed, and fresh telemetry supports no user-impacting failure with at least two independent healthy signals; volume or an isolated alert alone is not impact. | Close as noise with evidence; do not run remediation. |

**Impact-based classification (team decision).** Classify severity from measured user impact when it is sustained across fresh telemetry samples or is ongoing while its confirmed fault flag is active. Do not wait for a five-minute elapsed-time threshold. Re-evaluate when impact, evidence, or fault state changes.

**Evidence and duration rules.** Error rate is failed requests divided by total requests for the affected user path. Any broad critical-path error rate of ≥5% is evaluated over a rolling 10-minute window and requires at least 20 requests; below that minimum or with an unknown denominator, use `S3.0` rather than extrapolating the threshold. Latency is p95 and must be sustained across fresh samples, or tied to a confirmed active fault flag, to meet the SEV2 trigger; elapsed incident duration is not a gate. Duration is measured from the first supported bad sample, not the alert delivery timestamp, but the classifier accepts it only for input validation and does not use it to assign severity. “Broad” means a core checkout/browse path or multiple services; “partial” means a material route, region or cohort; “localized” means a non-critical feature or isolated resource. These scope labels must be backed by request/trace evidence. For unknown user impact, missing/stale telemetry, one sample, or conflicting signals, do not treat absent data as zero or claim NONE; use `S3.0` and continue investigation. A higher supported impact condition takes precedence.

Policy IDs emitted in results: `S1.1` broad critical-path impact; `S2.1` sustained or ongoing material impact; `S3.0` insufficient evidence; `S3.1` localized/below threshold; `S0.1` verified no impact and no confirmed fault. Severity labels use canonical `SEV1`/`SEV2`/`SEV3` form; the harness also accepts and normalizes hyphenated inputs such as `SEV-1`. Thresholds are initial project policy and must be reviewed against the pinned shop's healthy baselines before production use.
